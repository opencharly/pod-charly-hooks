# 2026.290.1114 — feat(marketplace): declare the strata family

## Summary

Declares the **`strata` family** in this repo's `marketplace:` entity, which is the gate that lets the family's skill exist at all.

`charly marketplace generate` reads the refs list and validates every referenced skill's declared family against this entity. `opencharly/layer-strata`'s skill declares `family: strata`, and the entity did not declare it, so the generator **refused and wrote nothing**:

```
charly marketplace: skill "strata-layer" declares family "strata" which the
marketplace entity does not declare (add it to the mar…)
```

The new entry sits in `charly.yml`'s `families:` map beside the other thematic product families and mirrors their exact shape — `category: images`, a one-line `description`, `keywords`, `profiles: [user]`. `images` is the category the four sibling product families use (`distros`, `languages`, `infrastructure`, `tools`); the value was read from those entries, not chosen by feel.

This is producer-first: the family must exist and be tagged before `marketplace` can bump its refs list and ship a regenerated corpus.
